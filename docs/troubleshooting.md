# Troubleshooting

What to do when something goes wrong during the night. Each problem is a box: click the
symptom to open it. If nothing here helps, close the roof if you can, and call the
[contacts](contacts.md).

!!! danger "Roof won't close"
    Call the emergency contact on the [home page](index.md) immediately. Do not wait.
