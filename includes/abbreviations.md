*[ROTSE]: Robotic Optical Transient Search Experiment

*[XMPP]: Extensible Messaging and Presence Protocol: how the pyobs modules talk to each other

*[FITS]: Flexible Image Transport System, the standard astronomical image format
*[OCS]: Observatory control system
*[OTA]: Optical tube assembly: the telescope tube with camera and focuser
*[SSH]: Secure shell: logging in to another computer from a terminal
*[LAN]: Local area network: the site network
*[DHCP]: How machines get their IP address from the router
*[RS-485]: A serial cable standard for long cable runs
*[I²C]: A short-distance bus between a computer and small sensors
*[EAF]: ZWO Electronic Automatic Focuser
